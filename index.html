FEATURED CASE STUDY

Blinkit Sales & Outlet Performance Analysis

Comprehensive analysis of 8,500+ sales records evaluating sales performance across product categories, outlet sizes, locations, and outlet types to optimize operational efficiency and item distribution.

8,500+ Sales Records Analyzed
$1.20M Total Sales Revenue
Supermarket Type1 Top Sales Channel
Tier 3 Outlets Highest Revenue Region

Dataset & Inputs

Processed over 8,500 transactional records capturing item visibility, item weight, fat content, sales figures, outlet establishment year, outlet size, and geographic location tiers.

Methodology

Data cleaning & normalization using Python and PostgreSQL. Built interactive Power BI dashboards utilizing DAX measures, parameters, and custom visuals to evaluate item fat content distribution and outlet performance matrix.

Key Insights

  • $1.20M Total Sales: Generated across all outlet types with consistent volume growth.
  • Channel Dominance: Supermarket Type1 recorded the highest revenue contribution among all outlet categories.
  • Geographic Distribution: Tier 3 location outlets outperformed Tier 1 and Tier 2 in total sales volume.
